*What we're building (one sentence)*
Sentinel is *one* AI agent on watsonx Orchestrate. It takes a suspicious-login alert, chooses which tools to call, explains the risk in plain language, and ranks its recommended responses, each with a reason. *A human analyst approves every action. Sentinel never executes anything itself.*

*:pushpin: Latest decisions (these override anything older you read)*
1. One agent, not four. Reuse the existing backend (`CyberShield_IV`) plus *one* new tool.
2. Recommendations are ranked best to lowest, each with a reason. Sentinel is decisive: no "probably" or "most likely."
3. *Failure rule (updated Oct 15):* 2 failed logins within *5 minutes* flags the case. Sentinel investigates and has a ranked recommendation ready. A flagged case with a Low score ranks step-up MFA first. The count lives *inside `assess_login`*, not in a 4th tool. :no_entry_sign: The 15-minute window and the "4+ failures" tier are *retired*.
4. Human approval is non-negotiable. Nothing executes without it.
5. Where the docs disagree, `docs/build-spec.md` and `agents/sentinel.instructions.md` win.
